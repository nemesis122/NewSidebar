# Sidebar by NemeSIS v3.01.2.0

Portable Windows x64 hardware-monitor Sidebar. **EXE and matching source ZIP are both attached.**

## Changed: temperature is the main number in the glass designs

The six glass layouts (Liquid, Frost, Aqua, Prism, Smoke Glass and Glass Tubes) now choose their large number with the same rule as every other design:

- **CPU, GPU and drives:** the temperature is the large number.
- **Devices without a temperature (RAM, custom values):** the first reading, for RAM the load.
- **Network:** unchanged, download and upload show their live traces.

Load and every other reading stay visible as capsules beneath the large number.

Also included from v3.01.1.0: voltages, clocks, memory sizes and link speeds keep their decimals (for example 1.350 V, 1.91 GHz, 13.9 GB, 2.5 Gbit/s).

## Download and update

Download **Sidebar-by-NemeSIS-v3.01.2.0.exe** for the portable app. **Sidebar-v3.01.2.0-Source.zip** is the matching source. Close the previous instance and keep `aurora-settings.json` beside the chosen EXE; your settings carry over unchanged. The EXE is unsigned. See **VALIDATION.txt** and **SHA256SUMS.txt** for test scope and integrity.
